# Quarterly Financial & Compliance Audit Report - Agricultural E-Trading

## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Scope
This audit report reviews the electronic clearing, escrow balances, mandi fee distributions, and farmer Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) transactions executed on the Unified Market Platform (UMP) for the quarter ending September 2026.

Total Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) settled through the platform reached INR 4,820 Crores across 162 operating APMC markets.

## 3. Compliance Observations & Risk Mitigation
1. **Escrow Bank Reconciliation:** All partner banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis) maintained daily automated zero-balance escrow sweeps with zero discrepancy.
2. **Trader Default Rate:** Less than 0.04% of total trade volume experienced buyer funding delays. In all instances, trader EMDs were successfully liquidated to compensate sellers.
3. **Information Security Compliance:** ISO 27001 and PCI-DSS compliance checks passed with zero high-severity findings.
